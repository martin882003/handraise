## Scope

Represent what the product is intended to achieve beyond what code can reveal. Own the product brief, users and jobs, desired outcomes, success signals, constraints, non-goals, glossary, priorities, product goals, repository roles and cross-repository product context.

## Limits

Does not infer architecture from source, generate component/front contracts, run agents or mutate implementation state. Imported or model-generated content remains a proposal until human acceptance. Product direction may constrain planning but cannot silently rewrite observed evidence or completed execution history.

## Agent guidance

Keep declared facts, imported claims, assumptions and unresolved decisions distinguishable. Preserve human-locked decisions across regeneration, surface conflicting sources and allow a complete manual path. Store accepted intent as concise, human-readable Markdown; keep drafts and source excerpts private and disposable. Ask only questions whose answers can materially change architecture, priority or acceptance.

## Territory

`.handraise/product.md`, future `src/product-direction/`, product-brief and goal contracts, product-to-repository relationships and product-level portfolio APIs. Guided authoring and portfolio presentation are coordinated with Client Experience; Repository Planning consumes accepted or explicitly selected draft intent.
